# Core minimum outlook — 2026-10-22

Requested after the known-mission journal delivery `db3ce07`, before the next
individual-companion slice. Engineering estimate: **90–95%, reference 92%**,
excluding all pending human tests. Approximately **3–6 substantial technical
slices** remain, with uncertainty for regressions/integration. This is work
remaining against a bounded gameplay scope, not measured lines of code, a
percentage of all CDDA features, or a promise of hours/tokens.

The minimum loop is exploration → resources → inventory/equipment → food,
water/rest and treatment → crafting/reading/basic item work → combat → canonical
save/reload and recovery, with understandable 3D input and feedback. Movement,
perception, external containers, quickbar, native crafting/consumption/treatment,
melee/ranged, reading/disassembly, basic feedback and live language now have
scoped deliveries. NPC conversations, trading and known mission history add a
first social loop, although trade/camps were not originally minimum-core gates.

Remaining technical emphasis: essential sources/transfers of liquids and item
uses still missing from the 3D interface; close actual survival-loop omissions
rather than adding every possible CDDA subsystem; then consolidated non-demo
playthrough, busy/recovery/save regression and populated performance checks.
The planned individual companion panel is the immediate next bounded slice,
not grounds to expand the definition of minimum core indefinitely.

Full vehicle systems, camps/base management, every mission/mod/EOC service,
advanced mutations/bionics, all weapons/surgery and final artwork are outside
this minimum. The complete approved game remains a much larger undertaking.
Manual acceptance stays separately in pending-manual.md; excluding it from this
estimate does not invent human PASS results. Historical M5.5 FAILED and full
SAVE-001 OPEN remain independent, unresolved conformance gates.
