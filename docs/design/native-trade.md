# Native NPC trade

Right-click a perceived non-hostile NPC and choose the native trade reply.
The two panels display that NPC's native sellable stock and the native player
trade inventory, including permitted nearby items, worn gear and contents.
Select an item and set its quantity, drag across panels, or Shift-click to
toggle the whole available stack. These gestures edit an offer only. Review
and confirm opens CDDA's own confirmation; No returns to the same offer,
Cancel exits without exchanging anything. Native confirmation may also warn
that the trader will remember less debt than the proposed balance.

Prices, desirability, credit, debt limits, ownership, source locations,
container restrictions and NPC pocket capacity come from `trade_ui`, its
presets and `npc_trading`; the client has no alternate economy or inventory.
Unit prices are informational: the offer total is independently calculated
by CDDA, including its rounding and contained-item rules. Friendly companions
use native free exchange. Native shopkeepers collect permitted stock zones
and distribute incoming goods through the ordinary native paths. Opening
trade invokes native restocking/drop-invalid-inventory once; cancelling does
not roll back these normal native opening effects. Queries do not restock.

The native selector is the offer model; its terminal windows are skipped
under the external UI provider. Item locations and serialized fingerprints,
finance terms and native price/denial checks bind a quote to its sources.
Container and contained-item selections cannot overlap. Invalid quantities,
stale views, changed sources and failed credit/capacity checks cannot commit.
Offers outside supported native integer totals are rejected explicitly.

Confirmation invokes the ordinary native trade transfer sequence on the owner
thread: player to escrow, NPC to player, escrow to NPC/stock, source cleanup,
credit/sold update and native speech practice. Charge removal now consumes
the exact selected native stack; the native pocket check tests the offered
charge quantity rather than the entire unoffered remainder. There is no new
trade save format, client-side item copy or invented time cost.

CWM2.29 separates proposal correlations from ledger-backed commit commands.
Trade ID, quote ID, command identity and connection generation fence stale
UI input. Pending duplicates wait; completed identical commits receive the
cached result after native effects. Conflicting command reuse closes the
connection. A lost delta requires full native resynchronization; same-session
resync can re-present a quote without restocking. Connection replacement
cancels an unanswered quote or confirmation, never replays a trade. An already
accepted native confirmation settles once, even if its reply cannot be shown.

The quote and confirmation pause simulation. Manual/threat/recovery pauses
block committing; the trade's own menu pause permits its explicit confirmation.
Other gameplay and language changes are blocked while the native modal trade
is open. Esc cancels a quote; it never confirms an exchange.

`./trade-demo.sh --fresh` opens an isolated native fixture with an NPC ahead,
a bandage and lamp for sale, two distinct player ammo stacks and starting
native credit. Relaunch without `--fresh` to retain this demo save. The fixture
is generated by the independently linked pinned-core reader with
`--create-trade`; see the delivery report for reproducible commands. Existing
worlds and character creation are unchanged.

This is a bounded trade slice. It does not certify every modded/EOC service,
shop zone layout or exceptional item combination. Journal/mission presentation,
companion orders and specialised service interfaces remain separate work.
Historical M5.5 FAILED and full SAVE-001 OPEN remain unchanged.
